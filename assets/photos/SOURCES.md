# Exercise photos

Every folder holds two frames of the movement: `0.jpg` (start) and `1.jpg` (end).

- Source: [free-exercise-db](https://github.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db) by yuhonas, `exercises/<Folder>/<n>.jpg`
- License: the Unlicense (public domain dedication)
- `walking-treadmill/` comes from `exercises/Walking_Treadmill/`. It illustrates the walk days.
- Changes: `inchworm/` was resized from 2000×1333 to 850×567. Every other file is unchanged.
- Each JPEG also carries its origin in embedded metadata (`impeccable embed-prompt --read <file>`).
